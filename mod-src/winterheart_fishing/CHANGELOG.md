# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - First release

Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47+.

### Added
- Rods catch the real fish in the water, with cast charging, a casting bar and casting animations. Ported from Simple Fishing Overhaul 1.1.0 without its netherite fishing rod and painting.
- Configurable catch items and an option to stop Butchery carcasses dropping from killed fish.
- Junk and treasure are off by default.
